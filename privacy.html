PRIVACY, EXPLAINED

你的系统，你的数据。Your system. Your data.

Toucharium 应用与本网站的隐私政策。Privacy policy for the Toucharium app and this website.

POLICY

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 22 日Updated November 22, 2026

负责人与联系方式：Toucharium 独立开发者，kobe_wang@126.com。Responsible developer and contact: the independent Toucharium developer, kobe_wang@126.com.

适用范围Scope

本政策适用于当前 Toucharium App 及其产品与支持网站。Ubuntu Touch 客体系统、其应用，以及你主动访问的第三方服务，可能另有隐私政策。This policy covers the current Toucharium app and its product and support website. The Ubuntu Touch guest, its applications, and third-party services you choose to use may have separate privacy policies.

App 中的数据Data in the app

Toucharium 不要求创建开发者运营的账号，不集成开发者运营的广告、行为分析或自动日志上传服务。客体磁盘、客体文件及 App 设置在设备本地处理和保存，不会由 Toucharium 自动上传给开发者。iOS 的设备备份、系统诊断与恢复功能可能按你的系统设置处理这些数据。Toucharium does not require a developer-operated account and does not integrate developer-operated advertising, behavioral analytics or automatic log-upload services. Guest disks, guest files and app settings are processed and stored locally, and Toucharium does not automatically upload them to the developer. iOS device backup, system diagnostics and restore features may handle this data according to your system settings.

麦克风Microphone

仅当你启用麦克风并授权 iOS 权限时，Toucharium 才将麦克风输入提供给客体系统。Toucharium 本身不将录音发送给开发者。客体中的应用如何使用录音，取决于该应用的行为与权限。可在 iOS 设置中随时撤销授权。Microphone input is made available to the guest only when you enable it and grant iOS permission. Toucharium itself does not send recordings to the developer. Guest applications’ use of audio depends on those applications and their permissions. You can revoke access in iOS Settings.

客体网络与第三方服务Guest networking and third parties

客体系统和应用可以通过设备网络访问互联网，可能连接 UBports 软件源、更新服务器、你打开的网站或其他服务。这些连接不属于向 Toucharium 开发者上传数据，但相应服务可能接收 IP 地址、请求内容及你提供的信息，并按自己的政策处理。The guest system and applications can access the internet using your device’s network, including UBports package repositories, update servers, websites you open and other services. These connections are not uploads to the Toucharium developer, but the receiving services may obtain your IP address, request content and information you provide under their own policies.

网站访问Website visits

本网站不加入广告、分析脚本、追踪 Cookie 或第三方字体，不设置用于追踪的浏览器存储。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，GitHub 可能为交付与保护服务处理 IP 地址、浏览器信息及访问日志。详情参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。This website adds no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or third-party fonts, and sets no browser storage for tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs to deliver and protect its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

主动联系与反馈Support correspondence

当你通过邮件联系开发者，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和你主动提供的附件，仅用于沟通及处理问题，不出售或用于广告画像。仅在支持问题处理和必要记录期间保留，可通过支持邮箱申请删除；法律要求保留的信息除外。公开 GitHub Issues 的内容可被其他人查看，亦受 GitHub 的政策约束。When you email the developer, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. These are used for correspondence and resolving your request, not sold or used for advertising profiles. We retain them for handling support and necessary records; you can request deletion by email, subject to legally required retention. Public GitHub Issues are visible to others and are also subject to GitHub’s policies.

你的选择与删除Your choices and deletion

你可以撤销麦克风权限、管理客体系统中的文件，或删除 App 以移除设备上的 App 数据。卸载 App（保留文稿与数据）与删除 App 不同；设备或 iCloud 备份中的副本需要另行管理。对于已发送给开发者的支持资料，请通过邮箱提出访问、更正或删除请求；对于第三方服务中的数据，请联系相应服务。You can revoke microphone permission, manage guest files, or delete the app to remove its local app data. Offloading an app while retaining documents differs from deleting it; copies in device or iCloud backups must be managed separately. Contact the developer to request access, correction or deletion of support data you have sent. Contact third-party services about data they hold.

政策变更与联系Changes and contact

若应用的数据处理方式发生变化，本页会更新并标注日期。关于本政策或隐私权请求，请联系 kobe_wang@126.com。If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a revised date. For questions about this policy or privacy requests, contact kobe_wang@126.com.

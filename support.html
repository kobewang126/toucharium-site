GOOD TO KNOW

开发预览版本Development preview

当前版本要求 iOS 27.0 或更新版本；性能及应用兼容性仍在完善。The current build targets iOS 27.0 or later. Performance and app compatibility are still being improved.

在哪里下载？Where can I download it?+

目前尚未在 App Store 发布。源码已公开，面向开发者的构建说明见源码仓库；本网站不提供 IPA 或系统镜像下载。Toucharium is not yet on the App Store. Source and build documentation are available on GitHub. This website does not distribute an IPA or system image.

如何使用 iPhone 键盘？How do I use the iPhone keyboard?+

先点 Ubuntu 应用中的输入框，再点 Toucharium 底部的键盘按钮。点客体画面可收起键盘。当前按键转换主要支持英文、数字与常见符号。Focus a text field in a guest app, then tap the keyboard button in Toucharium’s bottom bar. Tap the guest display to dismiss it. The current key mapping primarily supports English letters, numbers and common symbols.

切换输入法后没有生效？Why has my keyboard selection not applied?+

在 Emulator Settings 的 Hardware 下方找到 Keyboard，选择输入法并保存，然后彻底退出并重新打开 Toucharium。Ubuntu Touch 键盘仍属实验功能。In Emulator Settings, open Keyboard below Hardware. Choose a keyboard and save, then fully quit and reopen Toucharium. The Ubuntu Touch keyboard is experimental.

为什么有些应用较慢或黑屏？Why are some guest apps slow or blank?+

当前适配仍在开发，部分应用使用软件渲染来兼容虚拟显示。应用兼容性与图形性能尚未全部解决，请在反馈中注明应用名称和复现步骤。The port is in development. Some applications use software rendering for virtual-display compatibility. Graphics performance and application compatibility are not fully resolved. Include the affected app and reproduction steps in your report.

重启会丢失文件吗？Will restarting erase my files?+

正常情况下，客体文件和设置保存在 App 的持久化存储中。删除 App 或删除其存储文件可能使数据丢失；重要文件请自行备份。Guest files and settings normally persist in the app’s local storage across restarts. Deleting the app or its storage files can remove them. Keep backups of important files.

反馈问题需要提供什么？What should I include in a report?+

请提供 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、Toucharium 版本、问题步骤，以及经过脱敏的截图或日志。不要发送密码、私钥或完整的客体磁盘镜像。Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Toucharium version, reproduction steps, and redacted screenshots or logs. Do not send passwords, private keys or a full guest disk image.

更喜欢 GitHub？可以提交公开问题。请勿在公开反馈中包含个人信息。Prefer GitHub? Open a public issue. Keep personal information out of public reports. GitHub Issues ↗
